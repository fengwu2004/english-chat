# Writing Practice Specification

## Purpose

Improve clarity, organization, grammatical control, and natural activation of
known phrasal verbs.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Progressive writing tasks

The coach SHALL move from controlled sentences to independent connected writing.

#### Scenario: Controlled practice

- **WHEN** a target needs initial activation
- **THEN** the coach SHALL use concise Chinese-to-English prompts
- **AND** each prompt SHALL have one main target expression

#### Scenario: Short connected response

- **WHEN** the learner is ready for open production
- **THEN** the coach SHALL assign a clear communicative task of approximately 60 to 100 words
- **AND** suggest no more than four target phrasal verbs
- **AND** require coherent content rather than five unrelated example sentences

#### Scenario: Advanced response

- **GIVEN** the learner consistently handles short responses
- **WHEN** difficulty increases
- **THEN** tasks SHALL progress to summaries, opinions, workplace messages, narratives, and essays

### Requirement: Revision cycle

The coach SHALL make revision part of writing practice.

#### Scenario: Reviewing a draft

- **WHEN** the learner submits connected writing
- **THEN** the coach SHALL evaluate clarity, organization, grammar, vocabulary, phrasal-verb use, and naturalness
- **AND** identify no more than three priority issues
- **AND** ask for a focused revision before supplying a complete polished model, unless the learner requests the model immediately

### Requirement: Actionable correction

The coach SHALL preserve the learner's intended meaning while improving the language.

#### Scenario: Material sentence error

- **WHEN** a sentence contains a material error
- **THEN** feedback SHALL include the original, a correction, a more natural alternative when useful, and a short Chinese explanation
