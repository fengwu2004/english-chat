# Speaking Practice Specification

## Purpose

Improve fluency, response speed, and spontaneous use of known English through
voice responses or learner-provided transcripts.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Production before modeling

The coach SHALL obtain the learner's attempt before presenting a full model answer.

#### Scenario: Timed response

- **WHEN** a speaking prompt is given
- **THEN** the learner SHALL be invited to speak for 30 to 90 seconds
- **AND** the coach SHALL not provide a complete model answer first

### Requirement: Gradual support reduction

The coach SHALL reduce preparation support as the learner becomes familiar with a topic.

#### Scenario: Supported first attempt

- **WHEN** a topic is new or difficult
- **THEN** the coach MAY provide three content cues, short sentence starters, and two or three target phrasal verbs

#### Scenario: Independent later attempt

- **GIVEN** the topic or language has been practised
- **WHEN** it is reviewed later
- **THEN** the coach SHALL provide only the question and necessary context
- **AND** assess whether relevant phrasal verbs are recalled spontaneously

### Requirement: Fluency and accuracy separation

The coach SHALL distinguish delivery problems from language-form problems.

#### Scenario: Assessing a spoken answer

- **WHEN** an audio response or transcript is submitted
- **THEN** the coach SHALL assess clarity, hesitation, sentence construction, grammar, vocabulary, and phrasal-verb naturalness
- **AND** assess pronunciation only when usable audio is available
- **AND** provide a concise improved version suitable for shadowing

### Requirement: Retelling support

The coach SHALL prevent retelling from becoming memorized reading.

#### Scenario: Preparing a retelling

- **WHEN** a source passage is used
- **THEN** the coach SHALL reduce it to keywords or an idea map after comprehension
- **AND** ask the learner to reconstruct the message without looking at complete sentences
