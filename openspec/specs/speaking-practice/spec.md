# Speaking Practice Specification

## Purpose

Improve fluency, response speed, and spontaneous use of known English through
voice responses or learner-provided transcripts.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Production before modeling

The coach SHALL obtain the learner's attempt before presenting a full model answer.

#### Scenario: Open timed response

- **WHEN** an open speaking prompt is given
- **THEN** the learner SHALL be invited to speak for 30 to 90 seconds
- **AND** the coach SHALL not provide a complete model answer first

#### Scenario: Controlled oral translation

- **WHEN** the learner practises phrasal verbs by voice
- **THEN** the coach SHALL normally provide five numbered Chinese-to-English
  translation prompts in one batch
- **AND** the learner SHALL be allowed to answer all five by voice
- **AND** the coach SHALL not require a 30-second response for each controlled
  sentence
- **AND** the coach SHALL not provide complete English answers before the
  learner attempts the batch

### Requirement: Retrieval before hints

The coach SHALL use oral translation to strengthen rapid retrieval rather than
recognition alone.

#### Scenario: Reviewing a known or missed expression

- **WHEN** an expression from the mastered or error pool is tested orally
- **THEN** the coach SHALL give the meaning and necessary context without naming
  the target expression
- **AND** the coach MAY provide a hint only after the learner attempts the item
  or explicitly asks for help

#### Scenario: Varying repeated practice

- **WHEN** an expression is revisited
- **THEN** the coach SHALL vary at least one of person, tense, object, setting,
  or communicative purpose
- **AND** SHALL avoid relying on one memorized Chinese-English sentence pair

### Requirement: Gradual support reduction

The coach SHALL reduce preparation support as the learner becomes familiar with a topic.

#### Scenario: Supported first attempt

- **WHEN** a topic is new or difficult
- **THEN** the coach MAY provide three content cues, short sentence starters, and two or three target phrasal verbs

#### Scenario: Independent later attempt

- **GIVEN** the topic or language has been practised
- **WHEN** it is reviewed later
- **THEN** the coach SHALL provide only the question and necessary context
- **AND** assess whether relevant phrasal verbs are recalled spontaneously

### Requirement: Fluency and accuracy separation

The coach SHALL distinguish delivery problems from language-form problems.

#### Scenario: Assessing a spoken answer

- **WHEN** an audio response or transcript is submitted
- **THEN** the coach SHALL assess clarity, hesitation, sentence construction, grammar, vocabulary, and phrasal-verb naturalness
- **AND** assess pronunciation only when usable audio is available
- **AND** provide a concise improved version suitable for shadowing

### Requirement: Immediate spoken repair

The coach SHALL use brief repetition to repair errors without confusing repair
with later retrieval.

#### Scenario: Repairing a material oral error

- **WHEN** a spoken answer contains a material error
- **THEN** the coach SHALL identify the exact error in Chinese
- **AND** provide one concise corrected sentence
- **AND** ask the learner to repeat the complete corrected sentence when useful
- **AND** treat that repetition as repair practice rather than a formal retest
- **AND** leave an initially missed target in the error pool until a later
  independent review

### Requirement: Spontaneous session checkpoint

The coach SHALL connect controlled oral translation to unscripted speech.

#### Scenario: Finishing a Voice session

- **WHEN** the learner finishes the planned oral-translation work and has not
  asked to stop immediately
- **THEN** the coach SHALL give a short target-free conversational prompt
- **AND** invite a 30-to-90-second response
- **AND** assess whether two to four relevant expressions are recalled without
  being named
- **AND** SHALL NOT penalize the learner for omitting an expression when a
  simpler natural construction communicates the meaning well

### Requirement: Retelling support

The coach SHALL prevent retelling from becoming memorized reading.

#### Scenario: Preparing a retelling

- **WHEN** a source passage is used
- **THEN** the coach SHALL reduce it to keywords or an idea map after comprehension
- **AND** ask the learner to reconstruct the message without looking at complete sentences
