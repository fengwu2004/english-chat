## Status conventions

`Pending` means not yet attempted in the initial stage pass. `Correct` means
an independently correct, natural target use; `Missed` means the target needs
post-pass review. `Mastered` means the item has also passed a later-date varied
retrieval check without seeing its English wording. Immediate repair does not
change formal status. Keep initial-pass totals separate from review totals.

Only unlocked targets appear in the status table. Append rows when a new
stage is unlocked; preserve earlier rows and all evidence. Record review due
dates only after an actual attempt. After the initial pass, complete missed
item review, remaining later retrieval checks, and a target-free checkpoint
before unlocking the next ten. Deferred items are not eligible practice targets.
