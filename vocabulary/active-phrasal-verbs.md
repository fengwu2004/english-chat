# Common Phrasal and Multi-word Verbs

> A practical collection of common English phrasal and multi-word verbs, with
> no fixed numerical target. It includes all 150 items in the corpus-based
> PHaVE List, plus useful expressions for conversation, everyday life, study,
> and work. Items are arranged alphabetically, not by frequency.
